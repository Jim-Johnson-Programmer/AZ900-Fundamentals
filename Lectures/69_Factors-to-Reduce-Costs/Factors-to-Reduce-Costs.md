# Factors to Reduce Costs

Azure cost optimization usually comes from right-sizing, removing waste, and choosing the right purchasing model for each workload.

## Common Cost Reduction Approaches

- Shut down unused resources.
- Choose lower-cost SKUs where appropriate.
- Use reservations or commitments for steady workloads.
- Monitor spending and set budgets.

## Exam Focus

- Good cost management combines visibility, governance, and design discipline.
