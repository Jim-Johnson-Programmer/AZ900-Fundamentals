# Service Family Services

Azure services are often easier to learn by grouping them into families. Instead of memorizing every individual offering, AZ-900 is mostly about understanding what category a service belongs to and when that category is appropriate.

## Main Service Families

- Compute services run applications and workloads.
- Networking services connect resources and users.
- Storage services hold files, objects, and other data.
- Database services manage structured and unstructured data.
- Identity and security services control access and protection.
- Management and governance services help monitor, organize, and enforce standards.

## Why This Matters

- Azure has many services with overlapping names.
- Exam questions often test the family or purpose before the product details.
- Grouping services by family makes it easier to choose the right option quickly.

## Exam Focus

- Learn the purpose of each family before memorizing individual services.

### Azure Management Groups

If your company has a lot of subscriptions, you will need a way to keep track of access, regulations, and compliance for all of them. Over and above subscriptions, Azure management groups provide a level of scope. Subscriptions are organized into containers called management groups, and the management groups are subject to your governance rules.
