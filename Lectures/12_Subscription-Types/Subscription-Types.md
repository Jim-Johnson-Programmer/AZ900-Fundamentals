# Subscription Types

An Azure subscription is a billing and management boundary. Different subscription types exist to support different purchasing and usage scenarios.

## Common Subscription Models

- Free account or trial subscriptions.
- Pay-as-you-go subscriptions.
- Enterprise agreement or organizational subscriptions.
- Student, developer, or Visual Studio related benefits.

## Why Subscriptions Matter

- Billing is tracked at the subscription level.
- Quotas and limits are often applied at this level.
- Access and governance can be scoped to subscriptions.

## Practical Use

- Organizations may separate subscriptions by department, environment, or workload.
- Multiple subscriptions can exist under one tenant.

## Exam Focus

- A subscription is a billing boundary.
- Resource groups live inside subscriptions.
- Management groups can sit above subscriptions for governance.
