# Exam Introduction

AZ-900 validates foundational Azure knowledge rather than deep implementation skills. The exam focuses on core cloud concepts, major Azure services, security and governance basics, and pricing or support considerations.

## What to Expect

- The exam is designed for beginners, business stakeholders, and technical learners starting with Azure.
- Questions are usually scenario-based and test recognition of the right service, concept, or cost model.
- You do not need hands-on engineering depth, but you do need solid terminology and service-matching skills.

## Main Study Areas

- Describe cloud concepts.
- Describe Azure architecture and services.
- Describe Azure management and governance.

## Good Preparation Strategy

- Learn the difference between cloud models and service models.
- Memorize the purpose of major Azure compute, networking, storage, and database services.
- Understand governance tools such as resource groups, subscriptions, policies, and management groups.
- Review pricing concepts like CapEx vs OpEx, consumption-based pricing, and support plans.

## Exam Mindset

- Read each scenario for keywords such as global, highly available, serverless, private, hybrid, or pay-as-you-go.
- Eliminate answers that are too advanced, too costly, or designed for a different workload type.
- Focus on the service's primary purpose, not every possible feature.
