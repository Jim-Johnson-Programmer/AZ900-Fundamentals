# IoT Services

Azure Internet of Things services help connect, monitor, and manage devices that collect or exchange data. These services are used in manufacturing, logistics, smart buildings, and other telemetry-heavy environments.

## Core Ideas

- Devices send data such as temperature, location, or status.
- Cloud services ingest, process, and analyze that data.
- Central management is needed for device identity, updates, and monitoring.

## Common Azure IoT Services

- IoT Hub for secure device connectivity and management.
- IoT Central for a managed IoT application platform.

## Exam Focus

- IoT Hub is the foundational Azure service for device communication.
