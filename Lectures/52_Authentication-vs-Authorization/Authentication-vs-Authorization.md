# Authentication vs Authorization

Authentication answers who you are. Authorization answers what you are allowed to do. This distinction appears often in Azure identity and access questions.

## Authentication

- Confirms identity.
- Usually involves credentials, tokens, MFA, or certificates.

## Authorization

- Grants or denies access to actions or resources.
- Often uses roles, permissions, or policies.

## Exam Focus

- Sign-in is authentication.
- Permission after sign-in is authorization.
