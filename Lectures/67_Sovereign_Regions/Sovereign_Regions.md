# Sovereign Regions

Sovereign regions are cloud environments designed to meet specific government, legal, or national sovereignty requirements.

## Why They Exist

- Some customers require stricter data residency or operational controls.
- Certain governments or regulated sectors need dedicated regional compliance models.

## Exam Focus

- Sovereign clouds address regulatory and jurisdictional needs.
