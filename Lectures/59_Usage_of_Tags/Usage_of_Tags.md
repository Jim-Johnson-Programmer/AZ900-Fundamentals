# Usage of Tags

Tags are name-value pairs applied to Azure resources for organization, reporting, and operational management.

## Common Uses

- Track cost center or department.
- Mark environment such as dev, test, or prod.
- Support automation or ownership tracking.

## Benefits

- Better resource organization.
- Improved cost analysis and reporting.
- Easier filtering and governance.

Tags are name-value pairs attached to Azure resources for organization and management. They are commonly used for cost tracking, automation, and governance.

## Common Tagging Uses

- Track owner or department.
- Mark environment such as dev, test, or prod.

- Improve resource organization without changing the resource structure.
- Support policy enforcement and operational filtering.

## Exam Focus

- Tags are metadata.
