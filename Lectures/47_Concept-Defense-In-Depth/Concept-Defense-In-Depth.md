# Defense in Depth

Defense in depth is a layered security strategy. Instead of relying on one control, multiple protective layers are used so that if one fails, others still reduce risk.

## Typical Layers

- Physical security.
- Identity and access.
- Perimeter and network protection.
- Compute and application protection.
- Data protection.

## Why It Matters

- Attackers rarely face just one barrier.
- Layered controls reduce the chance and impact of compromise.
