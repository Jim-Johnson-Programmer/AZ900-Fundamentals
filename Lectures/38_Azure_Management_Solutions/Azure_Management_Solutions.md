# Azure Management Solutions

Azure management solutions help deploy, monitor, automate, and govern resources. These tools reduce manual administration and give visibility across subscriptions and services.

## Management Areas

- Deployment and configuration.
- Monitoring and alerting.
- Automation and change tracking.
- Governance and compliance.

## Examples

- Azure Portal for interactive management.
- Azure Resource Manager for deployment control.
- Azure Monitor for observability.
- Advisor and Policy for optimization and governance.

## Exam Focus

- Management services support operations across many Azure resources.
