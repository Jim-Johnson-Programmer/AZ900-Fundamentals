# Azure Cost Management

Azure Cost Management helps organizations monitor, analyze, and control cloud spending.

## What It Supports

- Cost analysis and reporting.
- Budgets and alerts.
- Visibility across subscriptions and scopes.

## Why It Matters

- Cost visibility is necessary for governance.
- Teams can detect trends and reduce waste earlier.
