# High Availability and Scalability

High availability means a service stays accessible even when part of the system fails. Scalability means a service can handle growth in demand by increasing or decreasing available resources.

## High Availability

- High availability reduces downtime.
- It is achieved through redundancy, replication, load balancing, and failover.
- Azure services often include built-in resiliency across zones or regions.

## Scalability

- Vertical scaling means increasing the power of a single resource, such as more CPU or RAM.
- Horizontal scaling means adding more resource instances, such as more virtual machines or app instances.
- Elasticity is the ability to scale automatically as demand changes.

## Why It Matters in Azure

- Cloud platforms make scaling faster than traditional on-premises environments.
- Many Azure services can autoscale based on load.
- Designing for availability and scale is part of building reliable cloud solutions.

## Exam Focus

- Know the difference between availability and scalability.
- Understand the difference between vertical and horizontal scaling.
- Recognize that Azure supports both manual and automatic scaling models.
