# AI Services

Azure AI services provide prebuilt and customizable capabilities for vision, language, speech, search, and machine learning scenarios. AZ-900 tests recognition of what AI services are for and when they are useful.

## Common Capabilities

- Image and video analysis.
- Speech recognition and text to speech.
- Language understanding, translation, and summarization.
- Knowledge mining and intelligent search.

## Service Categories

- Azure AI services for prebuilt APIs.
- Azure Machine Learning for building and managing ML models.

## Exam Focus

- Prebuilt AI services reduce the need to build models from scratch.
