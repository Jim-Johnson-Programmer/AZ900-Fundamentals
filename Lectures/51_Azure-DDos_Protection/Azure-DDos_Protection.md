# Azure DDoS Protection

Azure DDoS Protection helps defend applications and networks from distributed denial-of-service attacks that try to overwhelm resources or make services unavailable.

## What It Helps Protect

- Public-facing network endpoints.
- Internet-exposed workloads hosted in Azure.

## Key Idea

- DDoS attacks target availability.
- Protection services detect and mitigate malicious traffic spikes.

## Exam Focus

- DDoS protection is specifically about availability attacks.

- Automatic attack detection and mitigation.
- Better resilience for internet-facing workloads.

## Why It Matters

- Availability is a security concern as well as an operations concern.
- Public-facing applications may need stronger protection than default platform coverage alone.

## Exam Focus

- DDoS protection focuses on availability threats caused by traffic floods.
- It is different from firewalls, which filter traffic by policy.
