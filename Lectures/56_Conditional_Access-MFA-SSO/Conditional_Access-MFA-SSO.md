# Conditional Access, MFA, and SSO

Conditional Access, multi-factor authentication, and single sign-on are core identity security concepts in Azure.

## MFA

- Requires more than one form of verification.
- Reduces risk from compromised passwords.

## SSO

- Lets users sign in once and access multiple applications.
- Improves usability and can centralize identity control.

## Conditional Access

- Applies access decisions based on signals like user, device, location, and risk.

## Exam Focus

- MFA strengthens authentication.
- SSO improves user experience.
