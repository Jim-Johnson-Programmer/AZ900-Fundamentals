Azure services move through lifecycle stages as they mature. These stages help customers understand support expectations and production readiness.

## Typical Lifecycle Stages

- Preview services are available for evaluation and may have limited support or changing features.
- General availability, or GA, means the service is fully released for production use.

## Why It Matters

- Preview features may change and can carry more risk.
- Production workloads usually prefer generally available services.

## Exam Focus

- Distinguish preview from general availability.
- Lifecycle stage affects support expectations and deployment decisions.
