# Reliability and Predictability

Reliability means a workload can recover from failures and continue to operate. Predictability means performance and cost outcomes are stable enough to plan around.

## Reliability

- Built through redundancy, failover planning, backups, and resilient architecture.
- Azure regions, availability zones, and managed services contribute to reliability.
- Monitoring helps detect failures before they become major outages.

## Predictability

- Performance predictability means applications behave consistently under expected load.
- Cost predictability means you can estimate and control spending.
- Right-sizing and monitoring help improve both.

## Azure Perspective

- Azure provides SLAs, autoscaling, monitoring, and architecture options that support reliable outcomes.
- Cost tools help forecast and track usage.

## Exam Focus

- Reliability is about continuity and recovery.
- Predictability includes both performance and cost consistency.
- Cloud design choices influence both qualities.
