# Subscription Consumption vs Monthly Models

Azure pricing can follow different patterns depending on the service and agreement. The most common mental model is consumption-based pricing, but some subscriptions and licenses introduce predictable monthly charges.

## Consumption-Based Pricing

- Pay only for the resources you use.
- Common for compute, storage, bandwidth, and many platform services.
- Good for variable or unpredictable workloads.

## Monthly or Committed Costs

- Some services, plans, or agreements create recurring monthly costs.
- Reserved capacity or support plans may trade flexibility for lower long-term cost.
- Licensing arrangements can also affect the billing pattern.

## Why This Matters

- Cost optimization depends on understanding whether a workload is steady or variable.
- Consumption works well for bursty demand.
- Monthly or reserved models can reduce cost for predictable usage.

## Exam Focus

- Azure is primarily known for pay-as-you-go consumption.
- Some offerings can include fixed or recurring monthly charges.
- The right pricing model depends on workload stability and cost goals.
