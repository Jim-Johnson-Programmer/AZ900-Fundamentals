# Azure Service Health

Azure Service Health provides information about Azure service issues, planned maintenance, and environment-specific health events that may affect your subscriptions.

## What It Shows

- Service issues in Azure.
- Planned maintenance notices.
- Health advisories and subscription-impacting events.

## Why It Matters

- Helps distinguish Azure platform incidents from application problems.
- Supports faster communication and triage.

## Exam Focus

- Service Health is about Azure platform status as it affects you.
