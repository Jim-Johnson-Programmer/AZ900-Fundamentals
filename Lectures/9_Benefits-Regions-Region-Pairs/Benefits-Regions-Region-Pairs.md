# Benefits of Regions and Region Pairs

Azure regions are sets of datacenters deployed within a specific geographic area. Region pairs are paired Azure regions within the same geography that support disaster recovery and platform updates.

## Azure Regions

- Help place resources close to users for lower latency.
- Support data residency and compliance requirements.
- Allow organizations to choose where workloads run.

## Region Pairs

- Provide a second region for recovery planning.
- Some platform updates are sequenced across region pairs to reduce simultaneous impact.
- Certain disaster recovery strategies are easier to design around paired regions.

## Benefits

- Better business continuity options.
- Greater resilience against regional outages.
- Improved compliance and performance planning.

## Exam Focus

- A region is a geographic deployment area.
- Region pairs help support disaster recovery and planned maintenance strategies.
- Not every service uses region pairs in the same way, but the concept matters for resilience.
