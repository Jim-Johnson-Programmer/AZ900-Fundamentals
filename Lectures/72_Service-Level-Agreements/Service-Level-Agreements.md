# Service Level Agreements

Service level agreements, or SLAs, describe Microsoft's commitments for service uptime and availability.

## Why SLAs Matter

- They communicate expected service availability.
- They help customers compare service resilience characteristics.
- They are important for designing workloads that meet business requirements.

## Important Idea

- Combining services can affect overall solution availability.
- Higher availability goals often require better architecture, not just selecting a service.

- They set expectations for availability.
