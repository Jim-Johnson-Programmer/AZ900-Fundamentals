# CapEx and OpEx Costs

Capital expenditure (CapEx) is the upfront cost of buying and maintaining physical infrastructure. Operational expenditure (OpEx) is the ongoing cost of consuming services as needed.

## CapEx

- Large upfront investment.
- Includes servers, networking hardware, storage devices, and datacenter facilities.
- Capacity planning must happen before demand arrives.

## OpEx

- Pay for what you use.
- Costs are spread over time.
- Makes it easier to start small and scale later.

## Why Cloud Favors OpEx

- Azure removes the need to buy most physical infrastructure.
- Organizations can provision services quickly and stop paying when resources are no longer needed.
- Budgeting becomes more flexible because spending aligns more closely to actual usage.

## Exam Focus

- CapEx is common in traditional on-premises environments.
- OpEx is common in cloud computing.
- Cloud spending does not eliminate cost, but it shifts cost structure and purchasing behavior.
