# Governance Hierarchy Constructs

Azure governance works through hierarchical scopes that let organizations apply access and policy consistently.

## Main Levels

- Management groups
- Subscriptions
- Resource groups
- Resources

## Why It Matters

- Permissions and policies can inherit downward.
- Organizations can manage large environments more consistently.
